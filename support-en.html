Yugi DB help

First launch and connectivity

Download the catalog with an internet connection or restore an existing .yugibackup file. You can then read saved information and manage your collection offline. Images not yet downloaded and catalog and price updates require internet access. If a download fails, check your connection and try again.

Add cards

Find a card in Catalog and add it to your collection. Choose its variant and confirm quantity, language and folder. The scanner helps identify the card; review the result and select its printing or rarity when necessary. It does not automatically determine every variant.

Camera

Keep the whole card inside the frame. If permission was denied, enable camera access for Yugi DB in iOS Settings. Manual search remains available. Your custom photos are saved locally as references for variants.

Collections, favorites and wishlist

Folders organize the copies you own. Favorites and wishlist entries are separate from owned copies. Review the confirmation before moving or deleting items.

Prices

Values are estimates in USD from external providers. They may be outdated, have no match for your variant or not reflect a card's physical condition. Check the displayed source and edition: a generic estimate does not confirm the value of your variant. The app does not execute purchases or guarantee a selling price.

Create and restore a backup

In Settings, choose Create backup and save the .yugibackup file to Files or another destination. It includes your collection, folders, favorites, wishlist, saved catalog, custom photos, pending scan batch, artwork selections and preferences. Keep a copy somewhere you can access if you change phones. The file has no password or app-level encryption and may contain notes and photos.

To recover your data, choose Restore backup in Settings or on first launch and select the file. Review the date and summary before confirming. Restoration replaces current data without merging collections and creates an internal recovery copy of the previous state. If a problem is reported, keep the original file and review the message before trying another backup.

Downloaded reference images are excluded from the file and may need internet access when opened again. Custom photos are included. The app does not automatically copy the file to another device or synchronize your changes.

Export and deletion

CSV summarizes the collection you choose to export. It is not a restorable backup and does not include photos or all settings. Save a copy outside the app only if you want to share that information.

Personal-data deletion is available in Settings and requires confirmation. Check its scope before continuing. It also removes internal recovery copies. It does not delete backups or CSV files saved outside the app, emails or iOS backups.

Yugi DB privacy

Updated October 10, 2026.

Your collection stays on your device

Yugi DB stores your cards, quantities, variants, folders, favorites, wishlist, artwork selections, custom photos, pending scan batch and preferences locally. It requires no account and has no built-in service to synchronize your collection across devices.

Camera and photos

With your permission, the camera recognizes card text and captures variant photos. Text recognition runs on your device using Apple frameworks. Yugi DB does not send camera frames or your custom photos to catalog or pricing providers. Saved custom photos remain in the app's storage. You can revoke camera permission in iOS Settings and continue using the catalog and collection.

External connections

Catalog downloads and updates use YGOPRODeck. Reference images come from YGOPRODeck and image addresses returned by Yugipedia and TCGCSV/TCGplayer. Product and price queries use TCGCSV. These features require internet access when information has not been downloaded.

Requests may include card names or identifiers, set codes and product group identifiers to find reference information and images. They do not include your full collection, folder names, notes or custom photos. As with any connection, servers receive technical request information such as your IP address, requested resource, time and network headers. Each provider controls its own handling and retention; Yugi DB does not set or guarantee those retention periods.

Provider websites: https://ygoprodeck.com/, https://yugipedia.com/ and https://tcgcsv.com/. Downloading the catalog does not make every image available offline.

Backups and restoration

You can create a .yugibackup file in Settings and save or share it to a destination you choose. It includes your collection, folders, favorites, wishlist, saved catalog, custom photos, pending scan batch, artwork selections and app preferences. Downloaded reference image caches are excluded; those images may need internet access again.

The backup has no password or app-level encryption. Anyone with access to the file can read its contents, including notes and photos. Choose an appropriate destination and share it only with people you want to give access to. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file service, that service handles the copy under its own settings and terms. Yugi DB does not synchronize it automatically.

Restoring a file replaces current data without merging it. Before applying the restoration, the app validates the file and saves a local recovery copy of the previous state. That previous copy stays within the app until another restoration replaces it or you delete personal data.

Sharing and support

CSV exports are created on your device and shared only when you choose an app or destination in the iOS share sheet. An export includes collection information and, where applicable, folder names. Subsequent handling depends on the destination you choose. CSV is a reference summary and cannot restore the whole app or your photos.

If you contact support, your email and any information you choose to attach are received through the email service to address your request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. You can request deletion of the conversation using the same contact. Deleting local app data does not delete emails or previously shared copies.

Retention and deletion

Collection data stays on your device while you keep it. You can edit or delete individual items and use the personal-data deletion option in Yugi DB Settings. Its confirmation screen explains the scope; the catalog and downloadable cache may be retained so you can keep using the app. Deleting the app in iOS removes its local storage; offloading an app while keeping its data is a different operation.

Personal-data deletion also removes internal recovery copies. It does not delete .yugibackup or CSV files saved outside the app, support emails or iOS backups. Manage those copies at their respective destinations. iOS may include app data in system backups depending on your settings; downloadable caches are excluded from system backup.

Advertising and tracking

This version has no ads, advertising SDK, built-in usage analytics or advertising identifiers. The provider connections described above are still used to retrieve updated content.

Help website

The help and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add forms, advertising or visitor analytics. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. Read about its data handling at https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages and its policy at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Visiting that website is separate from reading this policy bundled in the app.
